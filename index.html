Clear bag policy & security

The University of Wyoming enforces a strict clear bag policy and security protocol for all football games at War Memorial Stadium to improve venue safety and expedite stadium entry. Under these guidelines, traditional large purses, backpacks, and diaper bags are strictly prohibited.

Approved bags

Every ticket holder, including children, is limited to bringing in one approved clear bag plus one small clutch purse.

  • Clear plastic totes: Must be clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC and cannot exceed 12 x 6 x 12 inches. Clear backpacks meeting these dimensions are also permitted.
  • Plastic freezer bags: A standard one-gallon clear plastic resealable bag (such as a Ziploc bag) is fully approved.
  • Small clutch purses: A small clutch or wallet is permitted alongside a clear bag, provided it does not exceed 5 x 7 inches.
